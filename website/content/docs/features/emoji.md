Search emoji and symbols, and paste one back into the app you were using.

Reach it with the **Search Emoji & Symbols** command or its own global shortcut recorded in
**Settings → Emoji & Symbols**.

## Actions

| Action                         | Shortcut                 |
| ------------------------------ | ------------------------ |
| Paste                          | <kbd>↵</kbd>             |
| Copy to Clipboard              | <kbd>⌘</kbd><kbd>↵</kbd> |
| Paste, keeping the window open | <kbd>⌥</kbd><kbd>↵</kbd> |

Pasting needs the [Accessibility permission](/docs/permissions). The footer names where the paste
will land.

## Moving around the grid

This is the one screen where **all four** arrow keys and all four
[Emacs chords](/docs/palette#emacs-chords) move the selection — <kbd>⌃</kbd><kbd>F</kbd> and
<kbd>⌃</kbd><kbd>B</kbd> step the grid here rather than moving the text caret.

Selecting into the first row scrolls to the top so the section header stays visible.

Mouse works too: click, double-click, right-click and hover.

## Frequently used

Tinycast keeps a persistent most-used section at the top, so the handful of emoji you actually reach
for stop needing a search. An emoji in that section also still appears in its own category.

## Skin tone

**Settings → Emoji & Symbols → Emoji Skin Tone**

**Default** · Light · Medium Light · Medium · Medium Dark · Dark

Applied wherever an emoji supports skin tones, including on paste.

This setting is one of the categories carried over by the
[Raycast importer](/docs/reference/import-from-raycast).
